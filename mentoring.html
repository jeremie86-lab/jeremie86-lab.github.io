Mentoring

Private photography lessons in Geneva, one to one. From your first frame to a way of seeing that is yours.

Book a first conversation

How I teach

I taught myself photography in the street, and that is still where I teach it. Each session lasts three hours, just the two of us: a little theory, then a lot of practice outside, in Geneva and around it. We start with your camera until it stops being in the way, then we work on what matters most: learning to see.

In 2019 and 2020 I led courses and street workshops for the Leica Akademie Switzerland, in Geneva and Zurich. Two chapters of the book Inspiration Leica Akademie (Heidi and Robert Mertens, Rheinwerk, 2020) are devoted to my approach: The Sneaker Zoom, on working with a fixed 50 mm lens and moving with your feet, and Beyond Routines, on questioning your habits to find new subjects.

Beginner or experienced, with a mirrorless, a reflex or a Leica: the lessons adapt to your level, your pace and what you want to achieve.

The programme

Step 1

The foundations

5 sessions of 3 hours · one to one · your own camera · Geneva and around

  1. Your camera. Menus, essential settings, focus modes, customising the controls.
  2. Exposure. Aperture, shutter speed and ISO: understanding the trio and going manual.
  3. Composition and natural light. The rules that work, when to break them, how to read light.
  4. Reportage in the city. In the streets of Geneva: anticipation and the decisive moment.
  5. Selection and editing. A Lightroom Classic workflow: import, sort, edit, export, back up.

Step 2

Then you choose

Modules of 3 hours · in any order · as many as you like

At the end of the foundations you receive a contact sheet, like the ones photographers use to choose their pictures. Each frame is a module. Circle the ones that speak to you and build your own path.

  • The street
  • The night
  • The event
  • Street flash
  • Travel portrait
  • Landscape and light
  • Architecture
  • Minimalism and black & white
  • Natural-light portrait
  • Directing a model in the city
  • Blur and movement
  • The artistic portrait
  • Finding your style
  • The series and the book
  • A module made to measure
  • A day-long photo excursion
