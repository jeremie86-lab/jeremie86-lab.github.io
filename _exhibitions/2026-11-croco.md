On one side, the paint gathers, raw, colour pulled straight from the knife. On the other, the grain of the flash fixes naked bodies in a black and white that forgives nothing. Two materials, two speeds, two ways of saying the same thing: animality, the wild and the raw in human beings.

The works advance towards one another and, at the centre, they bite. Red seeps into the metal, flesh curls beneath the matter. Out of this collision comes the crocodile: the only large predator to have crossed the ages without changing shape, it embodies a permanence our era, given over entirely to speed and obsolescence, has lost.

Koltes and Spierer ask a question that reaches beyond the studio: what remains instinctive, indestructible in us, beneath the layers of social varnish?

CROCO does not set two worlds side by side: it fuses them into a single organism, tender and ferocious at once, where eroticism returns to instinct and colour becomes wound again.

An exhibition with Galerie Fahid Taghavi. Visits by appointment.
