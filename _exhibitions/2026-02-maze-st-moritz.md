A boutique fair of around thirty galleries in the Belle Époque theatre hall of the Hotel Reine Victoria. The collaborative CROCO works, photographs on brushed aluminium worked over in oil, were hung with Tiphaine Koltes's paintings in the space supported by F.P.Journe.
