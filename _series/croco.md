Jeremy Spierer's black-and-white flash photographs on brushed aluminium, worked over in oil by the painter Tiphaine Koltes. Red seeps into the metal; flesh curls beneath the matter. These collaborative works sit at the centre of CROCO. Several were first shown at MAZE Art Gstaad and MAZE Art St. Moritz in early 2026, ahead of the exhibition's Geneva opening at Galerie Fahid Taghavi on 19 November 2026.
