A bollard, a manhole cover, a rose, a dog in a skull coat, a crumpled sheet. The most ordinary things of the street, cornered by the flash and pushed into hard black and white until they turn strange, almost indecent. First shown at Galerie Fahid Taghavi in 2022, alongside drawings by Tanya Molskaya.
